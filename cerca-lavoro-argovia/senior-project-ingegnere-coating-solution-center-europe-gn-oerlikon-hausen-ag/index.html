Responsabilità principali

  • Your Responsibilities
  • Acting as the primary technical interface for internal and external stakeholders
  • Managing multiple technical thermal spray coating projects simultaneously, ensuring priorities, timelines, and deliverables are clearly controlled
  • Preparing and approving technical documentation, including quotations, work plans, project documentation, coating specifications, technical reports, and QA documentation
  • Analyzing laboratory results and process data, and compiling clear, reliable final reports
  • Providing technical consulting support to internal and external customers
  • Planning and coordinating coating trials and campaigns
  • Collaborating with other Coating Solution Centers to share knowledge, project experience, and best practices

Requisiti principali

  • You hold a university-level technical degree and bring at least five years of relevant professional experience, supported by strong knowledge of surface technologies .
  • You have proven experience managing multiple international technical projects and are confident interpreting technical drawings, specifications, and related documentation.
  • Experience in thermal spraying is required.
  • You combine technical expertise with strong project execution skills.
  • You can break down complex technical projects into manageable work packages, delegate activities effectively, and communicate technical concepts clearly and concisely.
  • You demonstrate sound judgment, strong customer orientation, and good commercial awareness.
  • You are open-minded, initiative-taking, and comfortable working in an international environment.
  • You communicate effectively with customers, colleagues, and stakeholders, and you can motivate, coach, and support high-performing teams.
  • Good written and spoken English is required. We offer
