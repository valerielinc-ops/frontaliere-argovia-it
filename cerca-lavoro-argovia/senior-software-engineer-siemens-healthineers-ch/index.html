Descrizione completa

Join us in pioneering breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.

Our inspiring and caring environment forms a global community that celebrates diversity and individuality. We encourage you to step beyond your comfort zone, offering resources and flexibility to foster your professional and personal growth, all while valuing your unique contributions.

In this position, you will become member of a passionate team of highly competent software engineers and scientists developing next-generation products which will further increase the accuracy and confidence during radiation treatment targeting anatomies undergoing physiological motion. Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain algorithms for tissue tracking, patient positioning, and motion estimation in radiotherapy.
  • Design and evaluate conventional and AI-based methods for medical image analysis and tracking.
  • Optimize algorithms for clinical deployment using C++, CUDA, C# and Python.
  • Create realistic simulation of synthetic ground-truth data for quantitative algorithm validation.
  • Contribute to a shared codebase through code reviews, pull requests, testing, and documentation.
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to bring innovative algorithms into clinical products.

Qualifications

  • MSc or PhD in Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Physics, or a related field.
  • Strong software engineering skills and experience developing production-quality code in a team environment.
  • Excellent programming skills in Python, C++, and CUDA.
  • Strong background in algorithm development and numerical methods.
  • Experience with modern software development practices, including Git, code reviews, and CI/CD workflows.
  • Experience with machine learning and deep learning frameworks is a plus.
  • Background in medical imaging, computer vision, image registration, tracking, or computational modeling is advantageous.
  • Analytical mindset, scientific curiosity, and excellent communication skills.
  • Fluent in English.

What We Value

  • Scientific thinking and evidence-based problem solving.
  • Passion for developing innovative algorithms that improve patient care.
  • Ownership, code quality, and teamwork.
