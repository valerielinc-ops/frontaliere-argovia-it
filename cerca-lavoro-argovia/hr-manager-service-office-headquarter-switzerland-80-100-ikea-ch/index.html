Who you are

As the Service Office People & Culture Manager, you are the strategic People & Culture partner for IKEA Switzerland's headquarters and a key contributor to the national People & Culture agenda.

You ensure the effective delivery of People & Culture processes, enable leaders to grow their business through people, and strengthen collaboration across IKEA Switzerland.

Responsabilità principali

  • Your responsibilities

  • Ensure People & Culture processes are delivered consistently while continuously enhancing the co-worker experience.
