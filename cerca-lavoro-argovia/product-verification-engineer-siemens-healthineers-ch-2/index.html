Descrizione completa

Join us in pioneering breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.

Our inspiring and caring environment forms a global community that celebrates diversity and individuality. We encourage you to step beyond your comfort zone, offering resources and flexibility to foster your professional and personal growth, all while valuing your unique contributions.

Help shape AI-assisted verification for advanced radiation therapy solutions.

As a Product Verification Engineer with a focus on AI-assisted testing, you will play an important role in bringing high-quality products and solutions to our customers and ultimately to patients. You will verify that our treatment delivery systems meet their functional and technical requirements, combining hands-on testing with analytical problem solving and close collaboration with engineering teams.

You will work in an international, cross-functional R&D environment where verification goes beyond executing tests. Alongside your core verification work, you will help introduce practical AI-assisted approaches across the verification process, including requirement and test case reviews, and share effective practices with the team. Human oversight, product quality, and patient safety will remain central to this work.

Join our Varian's Business in Oncology Systems team and contribute to our mission of creating a world without fear of cancer.

Your Responsibilities

  • Plan and perform verification activities for our treatment delivery systems
  • Develop, maintain, and execute manual and automated tests based on requirements
  • Use AI tools for requirement review and test design, critically reviewing outputs
  • Evaluate, pilot, and share AI-assisted verification practices with human oversight
  • Analyze test results, report defects, and support troubleshooting with engineering teams
  • Contribute to requirement and design reviews and risk analysis

What You Bring

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in engineering, computer science, or a related field
  • Three or more years in software or system verification, preferably in medical devices
  • Practical experience using AI tools and the ability to recognize inaccurate AI output
  • A pragmatic approach to evaluating new technologies and their benefits
  • Strong analytical and communication skills, with the ability to support team learning
  • A strong commitment to quality and patient safety
  • Fluency in spoken and written English
  • German is an advantage
  • Proficiency in at least one programming language, preferably Python
  • Willingness to travel up to 10%

Nice to Have

  • Understanding of modern software verification practices
  • ISTQB, SAQ Certified Tester, or a comparable testing certification
